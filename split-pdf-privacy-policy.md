# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 9, 2026**

Split PDF ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Split PDF mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Split PDF is designed to help you split PDF documents quickly and easily.

We do **not** require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address to use the App.

When you select a PDF file, the App may access the file you choose solely to provide the requested PDF-splitting functionality.

## 2. PDF Files and Documents

Your PDF documents are processed on your device.

We do not upload, store, or transmit the PDF files you select to our servers.

We do not access the contents of your PDF documents for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose unrelated to providing the App's functionality.

Files created by the App remain under your control and can be saved, shared, or deleted using your device's available features.

## 3. Device Permissions

The App may request access to files or documents when necessary for you to select, process, save, or share PDF files.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

## 4. Analytics and Usage Information

We may use third-party services that collect limited, non-personally identifiable information to help us understand app performance and improve the App.

Such information may include:

* Device type and operating system
* App version
* Crash reports
* General app usage information
* Diagnostic information

We do not use such information to identify the contents of your PDF documents.

If third-party analytics or crash-reporting services are used in a particular version of the App, their respective privacy policies may also apply.

## 5. Advertising

If advertising is displayed in the App, third-party advertising providers may collect certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies. This may include device information, advertising identifiers, and general usage information for purposes such as delivering and measuring advertisements.

We do not provide advertisers with access to the contents of your PDF documents.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Because PDF processing is performed locally on your device, your selected PDF documents are not transmitted to our servers by the App for processing.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Split PDF is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for purposes such as analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other technical functionality.

These third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services used by the App.

## 9. Data Retention

We do not retain copies of the PDF documents processed through the App.

Any files generated by the App are stored on your device according to your actions and device settings. You are responsible for managing or deleting those files.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
