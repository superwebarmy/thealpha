Common Issues
PDF won't open

Make sure the file is a valid PDF and that it can be opened using another PDF reader on your device. Very large or unusual PDF files may also take longer to process.

PDF splitting failed

Try closing and reopening the app and make sure your device has sufficient free storage space. You can also try processing another PDF to determine whether the issue is specific to the document.

Can't save the split PDF

Check that Split PDF has the necessary file access permissions and that your device has enough available storage.

App is not responding

Close and restart Split PDF. If the problem continues, please contact us with details about your device and the issue.
